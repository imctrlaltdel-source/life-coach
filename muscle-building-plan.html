Key Rules for the Whole Plan

  • EXHALE on every single rep — on exertion (press, pull, push, drive). No exceptions for NPDR and hypertension.
  • Rep range floor is 8. If you cannot do 8 reps → weight is too heavy. Drop it.
  • Progress only when you HIT THE TOP of the rep range for ALL sets. Hit 15 reps × 4 sets → next session add weight.

  • 2-second eccentric (lowering phase) on ALL exercises. Controlled. Not flopping.
  • Never train to failure. Stop 1–2 reps before you think you might fail.
  • AM and PM sessions on the same day always use different muscle groups. No overlap.
  • Every 4th week is a deload week — mandatory. See Section 14.

4 · Conditioning & T2D Science

Why Both Resistance Training AND MAF Running?

Resistance training creates GLUT4 transporter upregulation — muscle contractions pull glucose from blood without insulin. Every set of squats, every pulling rep is a direct HbA1c-lowering intervention. This is the most evidence-backed intervention for T2D after medication.

MAF running (Maximum Aerobic Function) trains fat oxidation at low heart rates, improving mitochondrial density and metabolic flexibility. At HR 138 (your MAF ceiling), you're building aerobic base without cortisol spike, without glucose crash, without joint stress.

The combination: resistance training primarily improves insulin sensitivity in muscles. Aerobic training primarily improves cardiovascular efficiency and fat metabolism. Together, they attack HbA1c from two different mechanisms — the evidence for this combination is stronger than either alone.

MAF Heart Rate — 138 bpm (Corrected)

MAF Formula Applied Correctly:
180 − Age (37) = 143
Medical deductions apply when ANY of these are true: T2D ✅ + Hypertension ✅ + On medication ✅
All three criteria met → subtract 5 bpm
PJ's MAF ceiling = 138 bpm. NOT 143.
Start your first km at 12+ min/km. If HR rises above 135, walk. Patience at this stage = aerobic engine at Himalayan altitude later.

Post-Meal Movement — Why It's Non-Negotiable

A 10-minute walk after eating reduces postprandial glucose spike by 30–40% (research: Dipietro et al., Diabetes Care 2013). At HbA1c 6.8%, every postprandial spike is damaging retinal capillaries and kidney tubules. The walk is medicine — not a lifestyle choice.

Office options that count: Corridor walk, stairwell, parking lot lap, standing desk + calf raises. Minimum 10 minutes. Ideally 15.

Veg-First Eating Order — Why Before Every Meal

Eating vegetables first creates a fibre matrix in the stomach that physically slows glucose absorption from carbohydrates eaten after. Clinical trials (Imai et al., Diabetes Care 2010) show eating order reduces postprandial insulin by 38% and glucose peak by 29%.

Order at EVERY meal: All vegetables first (full portion) → protein → carbohydrates last. Never eat roti or rice before finishing the sabzi.

14 · 12-Week Progression & Deload Weeks

Context: With a protein ceiling of 80g/day and a caloric deficit, muscle hypertrophy is limited but real. The primary training outcome is fat loss with muscle maintenance + modest lean gain (~1.5–2.5kg over 12 weeks is realistic and meaningful). Every deload week is mandatory — the deficit and protein ceiling mean your recovery capacity is lower than a typical trainee.
Phase 1 — Weeks 1–3: Form + Tempo Foundation

Focus: Establish the 2-second eccentric on ALL exercises. Perfect breathing (exhale on exertion). Build the habit of tracking all 10 sessions.

Load: Start at current working weights listed in each session.

Rep target: Hit the TOP of the rep range before adding weight. Example: Lat pulldown 50kg — do 4 × 15 before adding to 55kg.

Circuit rounds: 3 rounds on all circuits (not 4 yet).

PM sessions: All 4 PM sessions active (Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri PM).

WEEK 4 — DELOAD (Mandatory)

  • Only 2 gym sessions: Monday AM (Shoulders) + Thursday AM (Legs)
  • Cut all working sets by 50%: if 4 sets normally → 2 sets only
  • Keep ALL weights IDENTICAL — do not reduce load during deload
  • No PM sessions this week
  • Running: reduce to 4km (not 6km). Keep HR ceiling at 138.
  • Calories: RAISE to 1,400 cal every day — this is the supercompensation window. Body rebuilds with extra fuel.
Phase 2 — Weeks 5–7: Load Progression

Focus: Increase load on exercises where you've hit the top of the rep range for all sets.

Load progression rule: Hit 15 reps (or top of range) on ALL sets → next session add 2.5kg (DBs) or 5kg (cables/machines).

Circuit rounds: Increase to 4 rounds.

Eccentric tempo: Maintained at 2 seconds — do not rush even as weights increase.

PM sessions: All 4 active. If one PM session was missed in Phase 1 → restart that session with Phase 1 weights.

WEEK 8 — DELOAD (Mandatory)

  • Only 2 gym sessions: Monday AM + Thursday AM
  • 50% sets at same weight
  • No PM sessions
  • Run: 4km only
  • Calories: 1,400 cal every day
  • Use this week to assess: any joints sore? any asymmetries? note them for Phase 3.
Phase 3 — Weeks 9–11: Volume Progression

Focus: Add volume (sets) on major compounds. Introduce antagonist supersets for efficiency.

Volume increase: Add 1 working set to all major compounds (4 sets → 5 sets on Lat Pulldown, Seated Row, DB Bench, Smith Squat).

Antagonist supersets (Tue PM): DB Bench Press supersetted with Seated Cable Row. No rest between the two — rest 90 sec after both. This builds density without shortening rest for either muscle.

Rest minimum: Maintain 75 sec minimum — do NOT cut below even in supersets. Hypertension rule.

Circuit rounds: 4 rounds + 1 extra exercise added to PM circuits.

WEEK 12 — DELOAD + ASSESSMENT (Full Recovery Week)

  • Walk only — no gym sessions, no running
  • 20–30 min walks, 3×/day (morning, lunch, post-dinner)
  • Calories: 1,400 cal every day
  • Weigh-in on Saturday with full HealthU+ metrics
  • Assessment: compare all metrics to baseline (Apr 25 + Week 12 reading)
  • This is the week to plan the next 12-week block

Injury Prevention — 4 STOP Signals

STOP THE SET IMMEDIATELY if any of these occur:
1. Sharp pain (not muscle burn — different quality, usually joint/tendon)
2. Joint pain (shoulder, knee, elbow, sacrum)
3. Visual disturbance during a set → STOP → sit down → check glucose. This may be hypoglycemia, not NPDR. Eat glucose tablet. Wait 5 min, check again before continuing.
4. Headache building during sets → stop, check BP if possible, drink water, rest.
TRAIN THROUGH — these are normal:
✅ Muscle burn in the last 3–4 reps (lactic acid — healthy)
✅ DOMS (delayed onset muscle soreness, 24–48h after session — normal and expected)

Sacral-Specific Protocol

Sacral Injury — Ongoing Management Rules
  • Glute bridge BW × 15 before EVERY leg session — non-negotiable activation of glutes to take load off sacrum
  • RDL: feel the stretch in HAMSTRINGS. If you feel it in LOWER BACK — weight is too heavy, reduce immediately and re-check hip hinge form
  • Any sacral discomfort → skip Thursday PM isolation session, replace with 30-min walk
  • 48-hour rule: if muscle is still tender to touch at 48 hours → do NOT train it
  • Bulgarian split squat: any right-left rep difference > 2 → note and report to physio at next check
