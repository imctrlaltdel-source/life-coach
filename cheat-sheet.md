| Step | Practice | Time |

2. Om × 3
3. Om Namah Shivaya × 108 on mala (8 min)

### Mahamrityunjaya — Full Text

> **OM TRYAMBAKAM YAJAMAHE**
> **SUGANDHIM PUSHTI-VARDHANAM**

*"We worship the Three-Eyed One (Shiva), the fragrant, the nourisher. As the ripe cucumber falls from its vine effortlessly, may I be liberated from death — for the sake of immortality, not more life."*
