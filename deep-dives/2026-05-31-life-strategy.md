## How TM Works — The Neuroscience

TM uses a meaningless Sanskrit bija as a vehicle for the mind to settle to quieter levels of thought until thought dissolves and pure awareness remains.

**The mechanism:**
1. Sit, eyes closed, silently introduce the mantra
2. Mantra is meaningless — the analytical mind can't grab it. No thought-trains.
3. Mind drifts toward subtler levels of the sound: audible → faint → barely-there → silence
4. As mantra refines, metabolism drops. Oxygen consumption falls 16–20% (deeper than sleep's 8%). Lactate drops 33%. (Wallace, *Science* 1970 — first peer-reviewed meditation study ever)
5. Result: **restful alertness** — body deeper than sleep, awareness fully present. EEG: frontal alpha coherence — both brain hemispheres in a single coherent waveform.

**Default Mode Network:** TM reduces DMN over-activation (rumination, craving, anxiety loops) — both during practice and progressively at baseline over months.

**TM vs. Mindfulness:**
- Mindfulness: observe thoughts. Brain in gamma/theta. Strengthens prefrontal regulation.
- TM: let thought dissolve. Brain in alpha coherence. Strengthens **autonomic regulation and cortisol baseline.**
- For your profile (BP, HbA1c, cortisol): TM's autonomic-restoration profile is more medically aligned.

**OM NAMAH SHIVAYA — The Panchakshara**

The central mantra of Kashmir Shaivism for householders. 2,000+ year lineage.

**Pronunciation:**

| Syllable | Sound | Element | Body location |
|----------|-------|---------|---------------|
