PJ's 7-Day Meal Plan

Target: 1,200 cal/day | Protein ≤90g | Carbs ~120g | Oil: 1 tsp/meal max

⚠️ HEALTH CONSTRAINTS

  • Kidney: Protein MAX 80-90g/day (Urine Protein 2+)
  • Diabetes: HbA1c 6.8% — fiber/veg FIRST, then protein, carbs LAST
  • NPDR (left eye): Exhale on exertion, never hold breath
  • BP: Low sodium, never skip meds

✅ RULES (every meal)

  • VEG FIRST — cucumber/salad eaten BEFORE every meal
  • 10 min walk after every meal (or lunges 4×15 at home)
  • Oil: MAX 1 tsp (5ml) per meal = 4 tsp/day
  • Dosa: Dry roast only — NO oil on tawa
  • Noodle/pasta: Max 50g dry weight
  • If protein >90g: Drop 1 egg white from snack

Eating order: Veg first (all 150g) → beans → rice last  |  Alongside: 200ml plain buttermilk (+36 cal)  |  With whey: add half scoop to buttermilk (+60 cal, +15g protein)

Why this works: No roti/paratha that can double. Rice is fixed at 3 tbsp and can't grow. Veg volume fills stomach first.

HOW TO EDIT THIS PLAN

Swap a sabzi: Replace any sabzi name + grams. Keep to 150-200g with 1 tsp oil. Calorie range: 90-180 cal depending on sabzi.

Swap roti ↔ dosa: 1 roti (85 cal) ≈ 0.65 dosa (85 cal). So 1.5 roti = 1 dosa.

Add/remove egg whites: Each egg white = 17 cal, 3.6g protein. Adjust snack count to stay under 90g protein/day.

Change guava: 150g guava (102 cal) ≈ 1 medium apple (95 cal) ≈ 200g papaya (86 cal). Avoid banana (90 cal but GI 62).

Noodle/pasta days: Always cap at 50g dry. Always reduce snack egg whites by 1. Always add 200g+ veg.
