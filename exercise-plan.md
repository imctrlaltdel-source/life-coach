## 8-WEEK RUNNING PROGRESSION (MAF Protocol)
**Starting point:** Apr 28, 2026 | **Base:** 7.33km long run, avg HR 142
**The rule every run:** HR stays under 135. Slow down or walk if it creeps above. No exceptions.
**Why it feels slow:** Your aerobic base is building. In 6-8 weeks your pace at HR 135 will drop by 1-2 min/km naturally.

| Week | Dates | Tue (Easy) | Thu (Moderate) | Sat (Long) | Total |
|------|-------|------------|----------------|------------|-------|
| 1 | Apr 28–May 4 | 25 min / ~2.5km | 30 min / ~3km | 5km | ~10.5km |
| 2 | May 5–11 | 25 min / ~2.5km | 30 min / ~3km | 5.5km | ~11km |
| 3 | May 12–18 | 30 min / ~3km | 35 min / ~3.5km | 6km | ~12.5km |
| 4 | May 19–25 | 30 min / ~3km | 35 min / ~3.5km | 6km | ~12.5km |
| 5 | May 26–Jun 1 | 35 min / ~3.5km | 40 min / ~4km | 6.5km | ~14km |
| 6 | Jun 2–8 | 35 min / ~3.5km | 40 min / ~4km | 7km | ~14.5km |
| 7 | Jun 9–15 | 40 min / ~4km | 45 min / ~4.5km | 7.5km | ~16km |
| 8 | Jun 16–22 | 40 min / ~4km | 45 min / ~4.5km | 8km | ~16.5km |

**What to expect by Week 8:**
- Same HR 135 → pace improves from ~11:30/km to ~10:00/km naturally
- Long run builds back to 8km comfortably with zero pain
- Shin pain resolves as connective tissue adapts at lower load
- VO2 max moves from 37.1 (Fair) → 39-40 (Good)

**Shin pain rules:**
- Before every run: 2 min calf stretch + shin stretch
- After every run: ice shins 10 min if any pain
- Pain during run → slow to walk pace, never push through at speed
- **Friday legs day → Saturday run sequence:** reduce calf raises on Friday to 2 sets (not 3) to protect shins

**Speed note:** No speed work for 8 weeks. Pure base building. After Week 8 — one run per week can include 4×2 min faster intervals.

## Progression Rule

When you hit **top of rep range across all sets** → add weight next session.
- Dumbbells: +2.5kg
- Cables/machines: +2-5kg
- Smith machine squats: +2.5kg every 2 weeks

## Left Shoulder Rules

- Never go behind the neck on any exercise
- Lateral raises: slight forward tilt, never raise above shoulder height
- Landmine press is ideal — angle protects the joint
- Sharp pain (not muscle burn) = stop immediately

---

## Medical Notes

- **Always exhale on exertion** — every single rep (NPDR/intraocular pressure)
- Never hold breath during heavy lifts
- Drink 500ml before every session, 1L replacement post-run
- Don't train to failure — kidney stress means moderate intensity is correct
- Post-meal 10 min walk on non-gym days = 30% blood sugar spike reduction
