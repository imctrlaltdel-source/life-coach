### Satiety Stack — Use These to Feel Full
- **Volume:** 150–200g cucumber before/during every meal. Zero calories, huge volume.
- **Mushrooms:** 200g raw = 44 cal only. Cook in any sabzi to double the volume.
- **Isabgol:** 5g before meals = forms gel in stomach, slows emptying, fills for 2–3 hours.
- **Moong dal cheela:** Higher satiety than sourdough at same calories — dal protein + fiber combo.
- **4 PM snack (non-optional):** Hung curd + guava + whey = 210 cal, kills 6–8 PM hunger that causes dinner overeating.
- **Dinner protein-first:** Eat egg whites/paneer BEFORE the cheela. Protein signals satiety faster.

### Dinner Late Rule (office delay past 8:30 PM)
If delayed: eat Option C only (hung curd + egg whites + cucumber). ~190 cal, 6g carbs. No cooking needed. Go to bed lighter.

### No-Besan Rule
PJ dislikes besan. Moong dal cheela is the primary cheela. Besan is removed from recommendations.

### Sodium warning
Cap cheese at ~20g. Skip papad/pickle. Flavour with lemon, oregano, chilli, ginger, garlic, cumin. BP.
