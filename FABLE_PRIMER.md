## What This Project Is

This is a 3+ month longitudinal life coaching record. An AI coach ("Coach") has been working with PJ daily since April 2026. The coach tracks:
- Every meal PJ eats (calories, macros, corrections for underreporting)
- Every gym session and run (from watch data, TCX files, screenshots)
- Daily step counts
- Emotional state, patterns, triggers
- Weekly body composition (HealthU+ scale)
- Habit streaks: gym, diet, steps, kriya (meditation), weed (he's trying to quit), parents call

The logs are raw therapist-style notes — unfiltered, honest, capturing what's said AND what's unsaid.

## The Coaching System

### Weekly Calorie Deficit Target
7,000 cal/week = ~1 kg fat/week theoretical maximum. Week runs Saturday–Friday.

### Calorie Targets by Day
| Day Type | Target |
|----------|--------|
| Gym day | 1,350–1,400 cal |
| Run day | 1,300–1,350 cal |
| Rest day (Sun) | 1,100–1,150 cal |
| Deload week | 1,400 cal |

### Calorie Correction Policy
- Home food: +20% correction (people underestimate by 30–50%, Lichtman et al. NEJM 1992)
- Restaurant food: +30% correction

### Protein Protocol
- Target: 75–80g/day (kidney ceiling 80–90g absolute max)
- Primary sources: egg whites, whey (10–15g/serving), curd, fish, chicken
- Problem in Jun 2026: averaging only 57–68g/day → muscle catabolism

## Key Behavioral Patterns (Identified from Logs)

### 1. The Collapse Chain (HIGHEST PRIORITY PATTERN)
**Trigger:** Weed use OR social event OR vacation
**Sequence:** Weed/alcohol → cravings → diet break → guilt → skip gym → identity slides → multi-day collapse
**Evidence:** Documented 5+ times in logs (Apr, May, Jun, Jul 2026)

### 2. Monday Late Wake
**Pattern:** Social/rest Sunday → can't wake Monday → misses morning gym → step count low → low protein day
**Frequency:** 3+ confirmed occurrences

### 3. Post-Vacation Collapse
**Pattern:** Returns from trip → 2 days legitimate fatigue → extends to 4+ days via inertia → gym streak fully broken
**Evidence:** Post-Munnar (Jul 7–10): 4 consecutive no-gym days

### 4. Snack Block > Lunch
**Pattern:** On no-gym days, unstructured afternoon snack becomes the largest meal of the day
**Examples:** Masala dosa + 2 vada (504 cal), nippattu + samosa blocks

### 5. Protein Neglect on No-Gym Days
**Pattern:** When no gym, protein drops to 30–40g (vs 75–80g target). No gym = no whey = no eggs = muscle catabolism
**Critical:** With urine protein 2+ and NPDR, muscle loss is both harder to rebuild and more dangerous than for healthy individuals

### 6. Travel Discipline Paradox
**Interesting finding:** PJ actually tracked well DURING travel (Munnar Jul 2–5) — double gym sessions, post-dinner walks, reporting every meal. The collapse happened AFTER returning. Travel itself isn't the problem; reentry is.

## The Core Tension

PJ is highly capable and disciplined in structured environments (travel with hotel gym, office days with clear routine). He collapses in transition periods and unstructured time. He has serious medical constraints that make mistakes more costly than for healthy people (NPDR means high BP during lifts could cause retinal damage; kidney constraint means protein mistakes matter; T2D means every high-GI meal matters).

He is not unfit. He is not lazy. He is a man with a complex medical profile, a demanding life, and a profound vision — who has not yet built a system robust enough to survive disruption.
